    When to pay

    If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year, the IRS wants a payment four times a year. These are the dates.

    Free products from brands

    Products a brand gives you in exchange for a post, review or other promotion are generally taxable income at their fair market value (usually what they'd sell for), even without a contract or a 1099. Something sent with no strings attached, that you didn't agree to promote, may be treated differently, and not every case is clear-cut. Log the ones tied to promotion under Gifts & freebies, and check anything in between with a tax pro.

    1099 Tracking

    Brands that paid you directly (check, bank transfer or direct deposit) above the yearly threshold generally owe you a 1099-NEC by January 31. Payments through PayPal, Stripe, cards or other payment apps are usually reported by that platform on a 1099-K instead, and only once you pass its reporting threshold, so not every payment comes with a form. You owe tax on all your income either way.
