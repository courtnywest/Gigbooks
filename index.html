    Don't forget PR gifts and free products

    If a brand sends you something for free in exchange for a post, review, or shout-out, the IRS treats that as taxable income at fair market value, the retail price, even without a formal contract and even if you never receive a 1099. Log these under Barter Income so they're counted here.

    1099 Tracking

    Brands that paid you $600 or more this year generally owe you a 1099-NEC by January 31. Giggy Up can total up who crossed that line, but it has no way to know whether a brand actually sent theirs, check these off yourself as they arrive.
