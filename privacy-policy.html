GigBooks ("GigBooks," "we," "us," or "our") provides financial tracking and invoicing tools for creators, freelancers, and small business owners. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely by our authentication provider — we never see or store your password in plain text).

Business and financial information you enter

GigBooks is built around the information you choose to enter, including:

  • Business name, address, and industry
  • Income and expense records, including dates, amounts, categories, and descriptions
  • Client names, email addresses, and billing addresses, if you save them
  • Invoices you create, including their contents and payment status
  • Receipts and other files you upload, and any logo you upload for invoice branding

Information from Gmail, if you connect it

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, GigBooks automatically scans incoming email for messages that look like payment notifications from a small set of known senders (for example, PayPal, Stripe, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle), so we can surface likely income for you to review and approve. We do not read, store, or display the full contents of your inbox — only the specific dollar amount, sender, and date extracted from a matching message become part of your GigBooks records, and every match is presented to you for approval before it's treated as real income.

Google API Services disclosure. GigBooks's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:
  • We use Gmail data only to power the income-detection feature described above, which is a visible, primary feature of GigBooks.
  • No person at GigBooks reads the content of your email. Our system's access is limited to the automated scan described above; a GigBooks team member would only ever view specific message content with your explicit, separate agreement (for example, if you asked us for help debugging a specific missed transaction).
  • We do not use Gmail data to serve ads of any kind, including retargeting or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not use Gmail data to train any general-purpose or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not sell or transfer Gmail data to third parties, except as needed to provide the feature itself (for example, our database provider, which stores the extracted amounts securely) or where required by law.
You can disconnect Gmail access at any time from Settings → Email Settings, and separately revoke GigBooks's access entirely from your Google Account permissions page. Disconnecting stops future scans; it does not retroactively delete income entries already created from past matches, which you can delete yourself like any other entry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide the core features of GigBooks — tracking income and expenses, generating invoices and financial reports, and (if enabled) detecting likely income from your connected Gmail account
  • Send you account-related emails, such as confirming your email address or helping you reset your password
  • Send invoices you create to the clients you specify, when you choose to email them directly from GigBooks
  • Maintain the security of your account and investigate suspicious activity
  • Improve GigBooks's features and fix problems

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your financial data or Gmail data to serve you advertising.

3. Sharing Your Information

With people you invite

GigBooks lets you invite an accountant, bookkeeper, or other collaborator to access your books, with a role you choose (view-only or full access). Anyone you invite and who accepts can see the information their role permits — this is a feature you control, and you can remove someone's access at any time from Settings → Team & Access.

With service providers

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate GigBooks:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication. Your account information and financial records are stored there.
  • Resend — delivers account emails (like confirmations and password resets) and invoice emails you choose to send to clients.
  • Google — provides the Gmail API used for the optional income-detection feature described above, if you connect it.

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function for GigBooks, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

For legal reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a Postgres database with row-level security enforced at the database layer — meaning access rules are checked by the database itself for every request, not just by our application code. Files you upload (receipts, logos) are stored with access limited to your own account and anyone you've explicitly given access to.

No system is completely immune to risk, but we design GigBooks so that even a mistake in application code cannot bypass the underlying access controls on your data.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and export: you can export your income, expense, and report data as CSV files at any time from within the app.
  • Correct or delete records: you can edit or delete any income, expense, invoice, or client record you've entered.
  • Disconnect Gmail: available anytime from Settings → Email Settings.
  • Remove a collaborator, or leave a workspace you've joined: available from Settings → Team & Access, or the workspace switcher.
  • Delete your account: available from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data, including any collaborator access to your books. This action cannot be undone.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted, not just hidden or deactivated.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

GigBooks uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in (the "Remember Me" option) and to remember a few display preferences on your device. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

8. Children's Privacy

GigBooks is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you directly.
